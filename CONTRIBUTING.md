# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Memory Catalog.

**Pull requests are currently closed.** To suggest a change, report a problem or ask a question, open an issue. Describe what you would change and why; the maintainer writes the change.

If pull requests open in future, every contributor will be asked to sign the [contributor licence agreement](CLA.md) once, on their first pull request. You keep the copyright in your contribution; the agreement grants a licence that lets the project be offered under both its open licences and a commercial licence (see [Licence](README.md#licence)).

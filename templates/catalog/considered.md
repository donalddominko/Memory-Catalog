aliases: [already considered, declined ideas, not discussed]

Declined = the user said no; the reason is in the user's own words, or "no reason given" (never invented).
Not discussed = a topic label only, never the assistant's conclusions; the reasoning stays in the chat at the checkpoint.
Adopted later → remove the line and log the adoption. Irrelevant later → mark "no longer relevant <date>".
